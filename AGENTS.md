## Flight searches

For flexible-date airfare searches, round-trip versus separate-ticket
comparisons, or layover and duration filtering, first read and follow
`codex_skills/flight-search/SKILL.md`.

Use `flight_finder_v2.py` as the primary engine. Treat `main.py`, `utilities.py`,
and `app.py` as the legacy RapidAPI implementation unless the task explicitly
targets that interface.
